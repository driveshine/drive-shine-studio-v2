FAQ

Common questions

Does the price depend on whether the car is new or used?+

No. Pricing depends only on vehicle type — Hatchback, Sedan, or SUV/MPV — not on whether the car is new or used. The same full checklist applies at every tier.

How long does an inspection take?+

Most inspections take 45–60 minutes on-site, covering all 300+ checkpoints across the six systems.

Can I be present during the inspection?+

Yes — you're welcome to be there while the inspector works through the checklist and to ask questions on the spot.

Do you inspect brand-new cars too?+

Yes, PDI for new cars before delivery is covered under the same tiered pricing by vehicle type — checking for transit damage, missing accessories and manufacturing defects.

What happens after the inspection?+

There's no separate written report to wait for — your inspector walks you through every finding on the spot, graded Minor, Major or Severe, explaining any problems along with the pros and cons, so you know exactly what to raise before you sign. Photos and videos taken during the inspection are shared with you for reference.
